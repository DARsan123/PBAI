# Demo limitations
SYNTHETIC DATA — NOT FOR CLINICAL USE. Demo only.

Out of scope: real database, real authentication, trial pre-screening, exports, LLM features, admin module uploads, KPI dashboard, clinical integrations and real alert delivery.

All seed records are downloaded to the browser and stored locally. Role gates and action checks are workflow simulations, not security boundaries. Consent controls research computation and display, not physical data isolation. Local audit records are editable and resettable. Production needs server-side authorization, secure persistence and tamper-evident auditing. Do not enter real patient information.

No clinical reviewer has approved these rules, option cards or emergency copy. Evidence links are reference context only. Eligibility means demo workflow completeness, never clinical suitability. PHQ-9 item 9 is a standalone demonstration, not the full questionnaire. No individual ranking, dose or probability is provided. Unknown interactions and hazards remain possible.

Alerts exist only in this browser; there is no real emergency dispatch, monitoring or notification service. Acknowledgment and resolution are simulated clinician actions. Signed follow-up is not a clinical prescription.

Research response flags are generated for an illustrative eight-week horizon. They establish no efficacy, causality, significance or medical discovery. Suppression is not validated anonymization. No regulatory clearance or privacy-compliance claim is made.
